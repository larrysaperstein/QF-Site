About Quick & Funny Musicals

We are the musical comedy team known for creating ambitious, ridiculous original and parody musicals... and putting them on stage before anyone can talk us out of it.

In 2026, we produced the fan-favorite Heated Rivalry: The Parody Musical, which celebrated 10 consecutive sold-out performances in Los Angeles and a massively successful Edinburgh Fringe Festival run.

We write, perform, direct, and produce each show we devise, together as a team. Oh yeah, and we do it all every month. We’ve developed a slate of wildly unique, delightfully weird, and downright hilarious original musicals that we can’t wait for you to explore and see on stage.
